## Recorded existing behavior

`XrayHero.tsx` dynamically imports `createXrayScene.ts` only when its hero slot is visible and the document is foreground. It currently excludes compact/mobile, reduced-motion, save-data and single-core devices from WebGL. Click, Enter and Space toggle face/body zoom.

`createXrayScene.ts` owns one orthographic camera and renderer. Root rotation increases by `delta * 0.42`. Body zoom is `1`; face zoom is `6.5`, targeted at `bounds.max.y - size.y * 0.08`. Camera damping is `1 - exp(-6 * delta)`; reveal damping is `1 - exp(-14 * delta)`. Resize framing is `max(size.y * 1.2, size.x * 1.16 / aspect)`. DPR caps are 1.5/1.25; cadence is 30/24 Hz. Pointer reveal uses a circular screen-space window of 17% of the shorter canvas dimension, with the original feather/discard shader. There is no drag control.

All anatomical layers will share the same normalized world coordinates and one root pivot. Mobile/reduced-motion eligibility will change only as needed to provide the newly requested lightweight/static-interactive anatomy behavior.
